## 📅 Versioning Policy

| Version | What changes |
|---------|-------------|
| `vX.0` | New phases, major restructuring, new capstone projects |
| `vX.1` | New modules within existing phases, large additions |
| `vX.2+` | Module updates, new sections, community feedback fixes |
| Patch | Typo fixes, broken links, outdated info — no version bump |

Modules are **not versioned individually** — check the git history for a specific file's change log.

---

## 🏛️ Maintenance Commitment

- **Issues** are triaged within 72 hours
- **PRs** are reviewed within 5 business days
- **Each phase** is reviewed and updated at least once every 6 months for accuracy
- **Deprecated content** is marked with a `> ⚠️ Note: This section covers [X], which was superseded by [Y] in [date].` banner rather than deleted — so old links don't break
